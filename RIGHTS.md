## Open legal question

The project is a ZScript mod loaded by UZDoom. Removing copied status-bar
source resolves the identified direct copying, but does not conclusively
settle whether any remaining code is legally a combined work with UZDoom.
The project-specific terms must be checked against any applicable GPL
obligations before public release. This notice makes no claim to license
third-party material under the project's terms.
