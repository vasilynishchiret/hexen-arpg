# Loot and equipment v0.5

An eligible Cleric kill has one shared 1/10 chance to create **one** ground item. Conditional on a drop, 40% is Sword/Axe/Hammer, 35% is Lightning/Frost/Serpent Staff/Sapphire Wand/Firestorm, and 25% is Chest Armor/Helmet/Bracers/Ring/Boots. Each group selects uniformly from its types. XP and +5 Mana2 are awarded independently of the drop roll.

Sword, Axe and Hammer preserve their existing damage roll: 80% common (0.65–1.10), 17% strong (1.15–1.80), 3% exceptional (2.00–3.50), rounded to whole damage. Magic uses the same roll against a base of 100, so its stored value is a spell power percentage (65–350%) applied to outgoing damage while that weapon is ready. These rolls are stored per instance in the ground actor, bag slot and equipped weapon. Magic keeps the original Hexen effects and their variable base damage. Armor and rings use `damage=1` only as a presence marker.

E picks up a visible nearby item into the first empty of 20 slots. When full, it stays on the ground. ALT opens and closes the inventory; mouse, WASD/arrows, Enter, and P manage items. There are three weapon slots and six equipment slots including Boots. A removed weapon slot is empty and cannot fire. Equipping a bag weapon changes the actual in-hand state and attack. Ring 1 and Ring 2 are independent, each granting +5 max HP. Current HP is clamped when rings are removed. Armor percentages are Chest 10% plus rolled stats, Helmet 5%, and ordinary Bracers 3%, capped at 45%. Native Hexen armor takes precedence if present, preventing double reduction.

Each generated Boot has one unique mobility affix 80% of the time, two 18%, or all three 2%. Bracers and Helmets may roll a special ability; special Bracers trade their ordinary 3% protection for it. Each new Ring rolls one passive effect. Two identical Ring effects stack with caps: Mana2 +2/12s, Vampirism at most 10%, Aura at most 4 damage/s. New Chest Armor rolls one to four distinct stat affixes based on common/good/rare tiers (80/17/3). It has no gameplay affix. The player data stores gameplay affix identity, equipment category, effect, power, duration, cooldown, and mana cost; stat affixes have parallel identity and power fields. Weapon damage type is reserved for a future iteration and does not alter attacks.

The player pawn owns the fixed bag and equipped-armor data; UZDoom saves actor fields with normal saves and Hexen hub transitions. On load, the existing weapon slot inventory classes retain their individual roll fields. Interactive save/load and hub verification is listed in [`TESTING.md`](TESTING.md).
